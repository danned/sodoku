## Full description

Sudoku is a calm, focused puzzle app built for quick breaks and longer solving sessions.

- Unlimited Easy, Medium, Hard, and Expert puzzles
- Notes, undo, redo, hints, visible conflict highlighting, and optional mistake auto-checking
- Automatic progress saving and offline play
- Clear local statistics for every difficulty
- Light and dark appearance
- Accessible labels and comfortable phone and tablet layouts
- No account required

Puzzles are generated and verified on your device. There are no ads, analytics, accounts, or remote puzzle services.

## Review notes

Gameplay is fully functional offline and no account is required. Puzzle progress, settings, and statistics remain on the device; the app contains no advertising or analytics SDK.
