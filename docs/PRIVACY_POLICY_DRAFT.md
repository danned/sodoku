## Data stored on your device

Sudoku stores the active puzzle, gameplay preferences, generated puzzle identifiers, and aggregate gameplay statistics locally on your device. The publisher does not receive this data. It can be removed from the app's Settings screen or by uninstalling the app.

In the browser version, this local save uses the browser's IndexedDB site storage. Private browsing or blocked site storage may prevent the save from surviving after the tab closes.

## Network use and tracking

Sudoku has no account system, analytics, advertising, cross-app tracking, or remote puzzle API. Puzzle generation and gameplay happen on the device.

## Children

Sudoku is a general-audience logic game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.
